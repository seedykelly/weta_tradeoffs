2. SYSTEMATIC DECLINE ACROSS ROUNDS (all individuals)
   
   Round 1 mean: 13.00 mm
   Round 2 mean: 12.93 mm (Δ = -0.07 mm, p = 0.65, not significant)
   Round 3 mean: 12.85 mm (Δ = -0.09 mm, p < 0.001, significant)
   
   This decline is small but consistent. Possible causes:
   - True biological change (unlikely: weta don't shrink between rounds)
   - Systematic observer drift or calibration shift
   - Different measurement protocol or imaging conditions in round 3
   
   IMPACT: This systematic bias reduces ICC below what random error alone would
   produce. If a fixed effect for measurement_round is added to the model,
   ICC would likely improve to ~0.85–0.90.
